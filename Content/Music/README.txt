Drop your songs here (import them in Unreal: drag .wav files into these folders in the Content Browser).

  Content/Music/Radio    -> every song becomes a truck radio station (R / T to switch, Backspace off)
  Content/Music/Ambient  -> background music, shuffled for everyone

Tip: in each Sound Wave, tick "Looping" if you want a song to repeat.
Only use music you have the rights to if you ever stream or share the game.
